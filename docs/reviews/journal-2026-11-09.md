## Lenses

### Don't lose big (Paul Tudor Jones)
**Verdict: breaks.** On 2025-10-30 the funded account lost 70R, which is 2.8× its whole
drawdown limit (scaled 2,429 against a limit of 867.5). Earlier profits kept the account alive;
a fresh account would have failed that day. The next-worst funded day, 2025-10-10, used 81% of
the limit in one session (−20.35R). Eval and forward-test losses stayed within −2.7R, so the
problem is specific to the funded account.

### Follow the system (Seykota)
**Verdict: breaks.** Days with a named model did far better than the two "Gamba" days:

| Strategy | Days | Mean day |
|---|---|---|
| ModelV2 | 26 | +5.08R |
| Forever Model | 13 | +1.70R |
| FVG | 3 | −0.44R |
| Gamba | 2 | −20.5R |

One Gamba day made +29R (2025-10-29, 20 trades) and the next day lost 70R (2025-10-30, 30
trades), against a funded average of 4.7 trades per day. The rule-free days carry the biggest
swings in both directions, and both were back-to-back overtrading days.

### Size from risk (Turtle)
**Verdict: breaks.** The funded journal uses one fixed risk unit (scaled 34.7), yet losing days
average −21.5R and reach −70R. Real exposure was many times the planned unit, so R stopped
measuring risk. In eval, the unit changed between two values (81.5 and 130.1) with no rule
behind it.

### Cut losers, press winners (Livermore)
**Verdict: mostly holds, with two exceptions.** Of the 18 losing trades outside the backtest (forward test, eval and
funded), 16 were cut at or before −1R. The two that weren't were both A+ ModelV2 trades: −3.5R on 2025-10-20 and −8.0R on
2025-10-22. Winners averaged +6.2R against a planned R:R of 2.6, so you weren't cutting winners
short. This lens can't judge adding to positions, because no add-on trades are recorded.

## Three biggest rule breaks

1. **2025-10-30, funded, −70R, "Gamba", 30 trades.** Breaks PTJ (don't lose big) and Seykota
   (follow the system).
2. **2025-10-10, funded, −20.35R, ModelV2.** 81% of the drawdown limit in one day. Breaks PTJ.
3. **2025-10-22, funded, −8.0R on an A+ ModelV2 trade.** A stop held 8× past the planned risk.
   Breaks Livermore.

## One change for next week

**Daily stop at −2R or 2 trades, whichever comes first, in every account.** That matches the
limits Apollo's strategy uses: at most 2 trades a session (spec 5.6) and the proposed −2R daily
cap (`risk-rules-review.md`, rule 1). To check it, every journal day should show `day_r ≥ −2`
and `trades_taken ≤ 2`. Then the backtest and your own trading follow one rule.

Only 9% of funded trades were journalled (8 of 94). Logging every trade is what lets this
review judge the next two weeks properly.
